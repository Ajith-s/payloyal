# PayLoyal merchant discovery script

Use this with cafe/bakery/salon owners before building too much.

## Opening

I'm building PayLoyal, a simple UPI payments and loyalty tool for local businesses. Customers still pay with their normal UPI app. The merchant gets sales insights, customer insight tracking, reward setup, and AI-powered reports delivered through WhatsApp.

## Questions

1. Which UPI QR/payment app do you use today?
2. Do you know how many customers are returning customers?
3. Do you check transaction reports in PhonePe/Paytm/BharatPe/etc.?
4. What information do you wish you knew about your customers or sales?
5. Would a daily WhatsApp report be useful?
6. Do you run any loyalty/reward program today?
7. Would you display a new QR if it gave you customer insight insights and rewards?
8. What would stop you from switching QR codes?
9. Would you ask a customer for WhatsApp number once if they wanted rewards?
10. Would you pay ₹299/₹49/₹999 per month for this if it worked?

## Success criteria

A good pilot merchant says yes to at least 3 of these:

- Will share current transaction exports/screenshots
- Will try a AI report
- Will test a new QR if payments settle reliably
- Wants rewards/loyalty
- Will pay after pilot if useful
