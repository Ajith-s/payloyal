What PayLoyal does

A customer intelligence layer for UPI-first merchants.

Most small businesses know money came in. PayLoyal helps them understand what happened, who came back, and what to do next.

UPI payment QR

Customers pay from any UPI app they already use. PayLoyal is built so customers do not need to install anything new.

Customer Insights

Understand customer frequency, visit patterns, spending behavior, and loyalty opportunities from payment activity.

Rewards setup

Create simple loyalty offers such as “5 visits = ₹50 off” or spend-based rewards that merchants can manage themselves.

AI-powered reports

Generate clear business summaries and recommendations, delivered in a merchant-friendly format through WhatsApp.

Built for real counters

How much did we sell today?Track sales, transaction count, average order value, and peak hours.

How it works

From UPI payment to customer insight.

  1. Merchant signs upAdd business details and complete provider KYC when live payments are enabled.
  2. Display PayLoyal QRCustomers scan and pay using PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM, or bank UPI apps.
  3. Analyze behaviorPayLoyal turns payment activity into sales trends and customer insights.
  4. Act through rewards and reportsMerchants can configure rewards and receive AI-generated summaries through WhatsApp.
